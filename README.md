# GANESH – THE JOURNEY

> ॥ ॐ गं गणपतये नमः ॥  
> *A beautiful, respectful, traditional Indian mythological and cultural adventure game inspired by Lord Ganesha and the timeless spirit of Ganesh Chaturthi.*

---

## 🌟 Game Highlights

* **3 Epic Worlds, 18 Fully Playable Levels** (6 unique levels per world).
* **World 1 (Mythology World)**: Sacred tales of Vinayaka's birth, his bond with Mushika, circumambulating Kailash, Chandra's lesson in humility, and the holy farewell. Concludes strictly with `MYTHOLOGY WORLD COMPLETE — 6/6 LEVELS` (No Level 7).
* **World 2 (History World)**: Verified cultural evolution from ancient Rigvedic hymns, harvest traditions, and Maratha patronage to Lokmanya Tilak's 1893 Sarvajanik community movement.
* **World 3 (Sculpture World)**: Meditative eco-friendly Shaadu clay crafting: kneading, shaping the sacred form, carving details, painting with organic turmeric and vermilion, and performing divine Aarti.
* **Multi-Device Adaptation**: Automatic responsive layout for Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, and Mobile across landscape and portrait orientations.
* **Seamless Multi-Input**: Supports Keyboard (Arrows/WASD + Space/E), Mouse (click & drag), and On-Screen Touch D-pad/Joystick.
* **Procedural Web Audio Engine**: Zero missing audio files! Indian classical ragas (Tanpura drone, Bhoopali/Hansadhwani harmonics), metallic temple bells (Ghantha), divine conch blast (Shankha), and modak chimes synthesized natively in real-time.
* **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5 Canvas + ES6 Modules. Runs instantly in any modern browser without npm installs or fragile build tools.

## 🎮 How to Play

### ⌨️ Keyboard Controls
* **Movement**: Arrow Keys or `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`
* **Interact / Action**: `Space`, `E`, or `Enter`
* **Pause / Resume**: `Escape`

### 📱 Touchscreen & Mobile
* **Virtual D-Pad**: Touch and slide on the bottom-left pad to walk.
* **Action Button (✨)**: Tap the bottom-right button to interact, inspect, or place sacred items.
* **Direct Touch**: Tap directly on characters, offerings, or puzzle options.

### 🖱️ Mouse Controls
* **Click** menus, dialogue choices, interactive objects, and puzzles.

## 🗺️ World & Level Overview

### WORLD 1 — MYTHOLOGY WORLD (6/6 Levels)
1. **Birth of Vinayaka (Puzzle-Solving Edition)**: Align the 3 celestial runes of Kailash, inspect 5 visitors with observation puzzles, and harmonize Lord Shiva's divine cosmic symbols.
2. **Mushikasura**: Investigate 3 garden clues, dodge rolling boulders in a fast-paced chase, outsmart with a fragrant modak, and guide Mushika through a narrow crevice.
3. **The Three Rounds**: Contemplate the cosmic challenge, complete 3 sacred pradakshinas around Shiva & Parvati with glowing trails, and solve the World-Family-Wisdom puzzle.
4. **The Celebration**: Gather 8 modaks and 10 hibiscus flowers, ask Mushika to hang golden garlands, light brass diyas, and offer the grand Aarti.
5. **The Moon's Curse**: Journey by moonlight, recover scattered modaks after a gentle mishap, teach haughty Chandra true humility, and grant compassionate redemption.
6. **The Final Journey**: Prepare offerings, clear the village path, assist pilgrims, and perform the peaceful, sunset Visarjan into holy waters.

### WORLD 2 — HISTORY WORLD (6/6 Levels)
1. **Roots of the Festival**: Uncover ancient Rigvedic epigraphs, harvest calendar connections, and match early symbols.
2. **Festival Takes Shape**: Discover Satavahana and Maratha court traditions, learn of Lokmanya Tilak's 1893 public renaissance, and sequence the timeline.
3. **Community Celebration**: Organize the Dhol-Tasha musical area, setup prasad counters, and align the 4 pillars of community cooperation.
4. **Journey Through Time**: Travel across 4 historical eras and collect representative cultural seals.
5. **Traditions Across Generations**: Explore regional traditions across Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Telangana/Andhra Pradesh.
6. **Living Heritage**: Curate an eco-friendly and charitable living heritage mandap, solve the master synthesis riddle, and celebrate community unity.

### WORLD 3 — SCULPTURE WORLD (6/6 Levels)
1. **Choose Clay**: Select eco-friendly river Shaadu clay and knead with pure water to optimal sculpting consistency.
2. **Shape the Form**: Assemble the lotus pedestal, stout Lambodara torso, head, and gracefully curved trunk.
3. **Add Details**: Carve large listening ears, the broken tusk (Ekadanta), crown (Mukut), modak, and Abhaya mudra.
4. **Colours and Patterns**: Paint with organic non-toxic pigments: turmeric yellow, vermilion red, sandalwood white, and gold filigree.
5. **Prepare the Celebration**: Sanctify the altar with mango torans, twin diyas, 21 modaks, durva grass, and fragrant incense.
6. **Completed Creation**: Behold the consecrated murti in divine radiance, perform the final Aarti, ring the temple bell, and receive blessings.

## 💻 Running the Game Locally

You don't need to install Node.js, npm, or complex dependencies!

### Option 1: Double Click
Simply double-click `ganesh-the-journey/index.html` or `index.html` to open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Option 2: Python Local Server (Recommended)
From the root folder, run:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then navigate to `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.

## 🚀 Publishing Online (GitHub Pages)

This game is packaged with zero dependencies and includes `.nojekyll` for immediate 1-click deployment on GitHub Pages:

1. Push this repository to your GitHub account: `lohithamanaswini10-ux`.
2. Go to **Settings** ➔ **Pages** in your GitHub repository.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)` or `/ganesh-the-journey`.
4. Click **Save**. Within 1–2 minutes, your game will be live at:
   `https://lohithamanaswini10-ux.github.io/<repository-name>/`
